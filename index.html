About this site

We are simply a group of people that have assembled with a common interest in mind, the high-revving 3.4 TDC/DOHC GM motor installed into the midengine Fiero. Every vehicle is different and this site is meant to be a central gathering place of the information.

The site was started in 2003 by Bob Neumann (85frankenstein) and DreXter from Pennock's. David restored it in 2008 and again in 2014.
